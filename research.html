Research

My research sits at the intersection of control, optimization, and machine learning, building toward embodied and physical AI. I develop principled decision-making frameworks—spanning robot foundation models, embodied AI agents, reinforcement learning, and optimal control—that bridge rigorous theoretical guarantees with high-performance adaptive intelligence. By addressing the fundamental trade-offs between efficiency, safety, and generalizability, my work seeks to empower autonomous systems—robots, autonomous vehicles, and large-scale mobility networks—to perceive, reason, and act reliably in complex, uncertain, and dynamic environments.

Research Directions

Robot Foundation Models

Foundation-level world models that let robots and agents simulate latent dynamics and plan under uncertainty.

  • Focus: Models trained on large-scale, multimodal data to capture the dynamics of agents, objects, and environments.
  • Approach: Combining generative modeling with model-based planning and RL to generalize across tasks and embodiments.

Embodied AI Agents

Embodied agents that perceive, reason, and act in the physical world, closing the loop between learning and control.

  • Focus: Long-horizon decision-making and coordination for physical agents under real-world constraints.
  • Approach: Uniting foundation models, planning, and control for robust, data-efficient embodied intelligence.

Reinforcement Learning

The theoretical and algorithmic foundations of offline and safe multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL).

  • Focus: Reliable policy learning from static datasets with formal guarantees on safety and robustness.
  • Approach: Bayesian inference and uncertainty quantification bridging reinforcement learning, optimization, and statistical decision theory.

Control & Optimization

The analytical foundations of optimal control, dynamic programming, and stochastic optimization for multi-agent and networked systems.

  • Focus: Structural properties, stability, and coordination across temporal and spatial scales.
  • Approach: Translating this analysis into intelligent transportation networks, distributed control, and autonomous-fleet optimization.
