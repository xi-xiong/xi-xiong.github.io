About Me

I am a Visiting Researcher in the Department of Engineering Science at the University of Oxford, hosted by Prof. Jakob Foerster, and a Research Professor in Transportation Engineering at Tongji University. Previously, I was a Postdoctoral Fellow at the Harvard Kennedy School. I received a Ph.D. in Transportation Engineering from New York University, an M.Sc. from Tsinghua University, and a B.Sc. from Jilin University.

Research Interests

  • Autonomous Systems
  • Robot Foundation Models
  • Embodied AI Agents
  • Reinforcement Learning
  • Optimal Control
  • Stochastic Optimization
